Been a While

Remember the last time.

Been a While is an iPhone app that remembers when you last did the small recurring things: changed your toothbrush, washed the bedsheets, replaced the air filter, had a haircut. It shows how long it has been and when each is due next. When you do it again, tap once.

No account, nothing leaves your phone

There is no sign-in and no cloud. Everything you enter stays on your device. Reminders are local notifications. Backups are files you choose to save.
