Support

Something not working, or a question the app doesn't answer? Start with the notes below; if they don't help, open an issue.

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub: github.com/Guksu/beenawhile-site/issues. You need a free GitHub account. The tracker is public, so please don't include personal information.

It helps to include: your iPhone model, the iOS version, the app version (Settings → About), what you did, and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

When do reminders arrive?
At 9:00 AM local time on the due day, or the number of days before that you chose for the item. Only items with a repeat have a due day, so items set to "No repeat" never remind.
I don't get reminders.
Check that notifications are allowed for Been a While in iOS Settings, and that the item has a repeat and a reminder other than "None" (open the item, tap the more button, Edit). Reminders are never sent for days that have already passed.
Does the app sync or need an account?
No. There is no account and no cloud. Everything is stored on your phone only.
How do I move my data to a new phone?
On the old phone: Settings → Data and reset → Save a backup, and keep the file (Files, AirDrop, mail to yourself). On the new phone: on the welcome screen tap Restore from backup, or later Settings → Data and reset → Restore from backup. Restoring replaces everything on the phone with the backup.
What is "Export history as CSV"?
A spreadsheet file of every completion. It is for your own use and cannot be restored into the app.
How do I delete my data?
Settings → Data and reset → Reset all data, or delete the app. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
What does "I don't remember" do?
The item is tracked from today without a made-up date. The first time you mark it done, that day becomes its last-done date.
